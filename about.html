About IPA Church Elappara

IPA Church Elappara is a Pentecostal church located in Shanti Nagar, Elappara, in the beautiful high ranges of Idukki, Kerala. As a local congregation under the IPA Church fellowship, with headquarters in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, our mission is to glorify God through worship, prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, and Christian service.

We are committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurturing believers in their spiritual journey, and serving our community with the love and compassion of Christ. Our church provides a welcoming environment where individuals and families can grow in faith, experience genuine fellowship, and encounter the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

At IPA Church Elappara, we believe in the authority of God's Word, the power of prayer, Spirit-filled worship, and the Great Commission to make disciples. Through regular worship services, prayer meetings, Bible studies, youth ministries, and outreach programs, we seek to help people connect with God and live according to His purpose.

Our vision is to be a Christ-centered church that impacts lives, strengthens families, and shines the light of Christ throughout Elappara, Idukki, and beyond.

"Worship the Lord in the Beauty of Holiness."
